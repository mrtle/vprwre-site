Eric Schmidt’s Anthropic bet may be worth nearly $10B.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 24, 2026, 9 a.m. PT

Former Google CEO Eric Schmidt invested in Anthropic, the company behind the Claude AI models, in its 2021 Series A, when it was valued at $461 million, according to PitchBook. He reportedly owns about 1% today, a stake worth nearly $10 billion at Anthropic’s latest private valuation of $965 billion, according to Bloomberg’s calculations.

He’s not alone. Spark Capital, the only VC firm known to hold an Anthropic board seat, put $75 million into the 2023 Series C at a $5 billion valuation; that position is now reportedly worth $7 billion, more than 90 times what it paid. Menlo Ventures, which led the Series D and has invested more than $1 billion over time, holds a stake reportedly worth $14 billion. ICONIQ’s $6 billion check last year, at a $183 billion valuation, is now reportedly worth $17 billion. Sequoia first backed Anthropic earlier this year at a $380 billion valuation, and its $3 billion for about 1% has already more than doubled. Sound Ventures, founded by Guy Oseary and Ashton Kutcher, put in $35 million in 2023 and later another $400 million, for a stake now worth billions. Kutcher left Sound Ventures this year to launch a new VC firm.

These are paper values at Anthropic’s last private price. Its IPO is expected this year at a valuation of as much as $2 trillion.
