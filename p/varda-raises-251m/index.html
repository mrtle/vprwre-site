Varda raises $251M

Varda Space Industries, an El Segundo, California, startup that processes drugs in orbit and parachutes them back to Earth, raised a $251 million Series D co-led by Lux Capital and Natural Capital, with Founders Fund, Khosla Ventures and General Catalyst among the participants. Varda told Reuters the round values it at $1.6 billion. Its capsules come home at Mach 25 and can double as hypersonic test flights for the U.S. military. CEO Will Bruey told Reuters the company’s biggest milestone will come around the turn of the decade, when it puts one of its “microgravity-enabled formulations” into a human in the clinic.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 6, 2026, 6 p.m. PT

FUN FACT: Varda’s first capsule spent about eight months in orbit, much of it waiting for landing approval, before parachuting into the Utah desert in February 2024, carrying crystals of the HIV drug ritonavir grown in space. Varda has brought four more capsules home since.

FOUNDERS: Will Bruey (CEO) and Delian Asparouhov (President; also a partner at Founders Fund)

INVESTORS: Lux Capital and Natural Capital (co-leads), Founders Fund, Khosla Ventures, Caffeinated Capital, General Catalyst, 8090 Industries, Giant Step, Also Capital

AMOUNT: $251,000,000

HQ: El Segundo, California
