Threatened, then hired.

Last year, Disney threatened this AI app. Now it’s hiring most of its staff.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 22, 2026, 10:21 a.m. PT

Character.AI filed for bankruptcy on Oct. 12. Disney’s opening bid: $20 million for a non-exclusive license to its tech and patents, plus job offers for 60+ people. Not the app. Not the brand.

In 2024, Google reportedly paid about $2.7 billion for a similar deal.

Disney isn’t buying an app. It’s buying a team.
