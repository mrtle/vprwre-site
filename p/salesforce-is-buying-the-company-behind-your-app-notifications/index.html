Salesforce is buying the company behind your app notifications.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 21, 2026, 11 a.m. PT

Salesforce agreed to acquire Braze for $47.50 a share in cash, an enterprise value of about $5.66 billion. Braze is the software brands use to send push notifications, emails and in-app messages, and it powers trillions of messages a year for about 2,770 customers, including Canva, Dairy Queen, Equinox, Instacart, Urban Outfitters and Venmo.

Salesforce plans to combine Braze with its Marketing Cloud, CRM and Data 360 and connect it to Agentforce, its AI agents. “Braze was built to turn live customer behavior into personalized engagement at scale,” said Patrick Stokes, Salesforce’s president of applications and marketing. Salesforce says Braze, led by co-founders Bill Magnuson and Jon Hyman, would bring its expertise to Marketing Cloud.

Braze started in 2011 as Appboy, went public on Nasdaq in 2021 and is based in New York. The deal needs approval from Braze shareholders and regulators and is expected to close in Salesforce’s fiscal year 2028.
