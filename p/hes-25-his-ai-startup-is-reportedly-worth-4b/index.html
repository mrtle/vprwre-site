He’s 25, and his AI startup is reportedly worth $4 billion.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 5, 2026, 9:30 p.m. PT

Ali Ansari moved from Tehran to Los Angeles at 10 and founded Micro1 at UC Berkeley in 2022. On Sept 22, Forbes reported a $100M+ round at a $4B valuation, citing two people familiar with the deal. Micro1 declined to comment.

The twist: it started as an AI recruiter that interviewed engineers for him. Then a client asked for 600 engineers in three weeks, Inc. reports, and he pivoted. Micro1 now pays doctors, lawyers and engineers to make the data that trains AI.

About $7M a year in early 2025. A $500M gross run rate by August, per TechCrunch, which puts net at $150M–$200M.

Our take: the strangest customer request may be your real business.

Would you bet a working business on one customer’s strange ask?

Micro1 sells AI training data to frontier labs it doesn’t name; no report we found names Anthropic.
