OpenAI is reportedly raising at a $1.25 trillion valuation.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 23, 2026, 4 p.m. PT

OpenAI is working on raising up to $30 billion at a $1.25 trillion pre-money valuation, led by Coatue and investors from the UAE, CNBC reported. Terms are still being negotiated, and the UAE funding could be tied to a deal for more computing capacity. OpenAI declined to comment. About a month ago, Bloomberg reported OpenAI was raising $30 billion at a $1.4 trillion valuation.

Its March raise valued it at $852 billion post-money, and it confidentially filed for an IPO in June. Rival Anthropic raised $65 billion at a $965 billion valuation in May and also filed confidentially in June, and CNBC reports it’s seeking a $1.5 trillion to $2 trillion valuation for its public debut.
