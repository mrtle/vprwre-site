What made Bernie and Bannon team up against AI?

What made Bernie Sanders and Steve Bannon team up against AI? They don’t agree on much, but on Sept 15 they agreed on this.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 3, 2026, 12:30 p.m. PT

Both spoke at the Pro-Human Assembly in Washington. Bannon on the AI labs: “We’re not doomers. But we aren’t gonna give these guys carte blanche.” Sanders, who had asked Anthropic, Meta and OpenAI to pause: “Regulating AI is not a progressive issue. It is not a conservative issue.”

The day before, Trump posted at least seven times about AI. One called AI doom a HOAX. Another: “Don’t kill the Golden Goose!”

On Sept 23, Sanders and Rep. Greg Casar filed a bill to ban superintelligence, with up to 20 years in prison. A UMass poll found 11% support an AI data center in their own community; 65% oppose. Republicans say no nearly 3 to 1. On Sept 29, Speaker Mike Johnson blamed the opposition on China.

Our take: nobody owns this vote yet. Trump is betting on the industry. Whoever speaks for the 65% who say “not here” could win a new kind of voter.

On the record Oct 2, 2026

By the end of 2027, a sitting Republican senator sponsors or cosponsors a new bill to pause or ban frontier AI.

The new divide isn’t left vs. right. It’s human vs. machine.

Which side are you on?
