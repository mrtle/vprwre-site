SoftBank’s first bet on Jack Ma was $20 million. By 2019, its Alibaba stake was worth nearly $135 billion.

Jack Ma has said his first meeting with SoftBank’s founder lasted six minutes.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 6, 2026, 3:30 p.m. PT

Ma’s young startup, Alibaba, needed money, and SoftBank founder Masayoshi Son wanted in. By Son’s own telling, he first offered $50 million, and Ma said that was too much. In January 2000, they settled on $20 million.

SoftBank kept investing and led an $82 million round in 2004. It didn’t sell a single Alibaba share for 16 years. By 2019, its stake was worth nearly $135 billion, CNN reported.

Then the cash-out began. By 2023, SoftBank held less than 0.5% of Alibaba, by one estimate.

Our take: Son backed a founder, not a spreadsheet, and held on for 16 years. Then SoftBank’s own losses pushed it to sell almost all of it.
