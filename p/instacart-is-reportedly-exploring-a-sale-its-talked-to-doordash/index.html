Instacart is reportedly exploring a sale. It’s talked to DoorDash.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 25, 2026, 10 a.m. PT

Instacart has hired an investment bank to evaluate its options and has held acquisition talks with DoorDash, The Information reported. Bloomberg reported that Instacart also talked with Uber, which reportedly walked away, and said the discussions are early and might not result in a sale.

Instacart’s shares rose more than 9% on Friday after the reports, while Uber and DoorDash shares fell. Instacart has a market cap of more than $13 billion, and its stock is up 25% this year. An Instacart spokesperson declined to comment.
