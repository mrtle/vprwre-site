Days after US restrictions, DeepSeek reportedly raised $15 billion.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 23, 2026, 1 p.m. PT

DeepSeek has raised about 100 billion yuan, or roughly $15 billion, Bloomberg reported, in one of China’s largest funding rounds. It came two days after the US ordered federal civilian agencies to remove DeepSeek’s AI from their systems over security concerns. Potential investors reportedly include battery maker CATL and Tencent.

The Hangzhou-based lab raised more than $7 billion in June at a $50 billion valuation, and was reportedly seeking $1.5 billion at about $71 billion in July. It’s reportedly spending more on AI infrastructure and open models, and a China IPO could come as early as next year. Founder Liang Wenfeng, who started DeepSeek in 2023, reportedly holds about 81.6% of its Chinese operating entity.
