Lawyers made this AI startup worth $8.8 billion.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 22, 2026, 6 p.m. PT

Legora, which builds AI agents for legal work, raised a $550 million Series E at an $8.8 billion post-money valuation, up from $5.6 billion at its previous round. Atreides Management led, with new investors including Baron Capital, Capital Group, Fidelity, Glade Brook and J.P. Morgan, and every major existing investor, including Accel, Benchmark and General Catalyst, also participating.

Its annual recurring revenue went from $1 million to $100 million in under 18 months, and the next $100 million took less than six. It’s now past $200 million. More than 140,000 legal professionals at more than 2,400 law firms and in-house legal teams use it, including more than half of the AmLaw 50, and the US is now its largest market by revenue.

CEO Max Junestrand co-founded Legora in 2023. It now has more than 1,000 employees across 18 offices.
