Amazon is backing a plan for 12 small nuclear reactors at an old Indiana coal plant site.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 20, 2026, 6:17 a.m. PT

Amazon, the utility AEP and reactor maker X-energy said Monday they are exploring a 960-megawatt nuclear plant at or near the former Breed coal plant in Sullivan County, Indiana, which shut down in 1994. Amazon is funding the development work and would buy the power under a long-term contract if the plant is built. No cost or timeline has been announced.

The plan calls for 12 of X-energy’s Xe-100 reactors, 80 megawatts each, in three groups of four. They’re cooled by helium instead of water, and their fuel comes in graphite “pebbles” packed with thousands of tiny coated uranium kernels.

If it moves ahead, Amazon says the project would create more than 1,000 construction jobs and more than 100 permanent ones. It’s the second project in Amazon and X-energy’s goal of adding more than 5 gigawatts of new US nuclear power by 2039.
