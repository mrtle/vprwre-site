He quit rocket engines. Now his startup is worth $750M.

He gave up on rocket engines. Now SpaceX’s backers value his startup at $750M.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 6, 2026, 1:30 a.m. PT

Pari Singh, a mechanical engineer who grew up in London, and his team were building rocket engines. Short on test gear and funding in the UK, they turned their design method into software: Flow Engineering. At 14, a business deal paid him just enough that he thought he’d “sold out,” he told Fortune.

The twist: Flow’s $50M round, announced Sept 30, was co-led by Antonio Gracias of Valor and Gavin Baker of Atreides, both Tesla investors who also back SpaceX. Sequoia came back.

Flow’s AI agents watch a hardware team’s CAD, code, simulations and tests, and flag what each change breaks. The company says its users at Rivian went from 40 to 1,500 in seven months. Revenue isn’t disclosed.

Our take: sell the method, not the rocket.
