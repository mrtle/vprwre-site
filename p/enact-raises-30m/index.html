Enact raises $30M

Enact Intelligence, a Swiss startup building force-and-touch sensing technology for general-purpose robots, emerged from stealth with a $30 million pre-seed round. Investors include Redalpine, Notion Capital and Firstminute Capital, and angels include Mistral CEO Arthur Mensch, Meta’s former chief AI scientist Yann LeCun and Hugging Face’s Thomas Wolf. Instead of relying on vision, its robots “sense force and touch through their own actuators, so they feel what’s happening in real time,” says CEO and co-founder Manuel Yves Galliker.
