Is your power bill paying for AI?

In Chicago, a consumer watchdog blames data centers.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 5, 2026, 8:30 p.m. PT

Illinois’ Citizens Utility Board says ComEd’s supply price is about 53% higher than two years ago and blames data-center demand. On Sept 23, Chicago Mayor Brandon Johnson proposed a one-year freeze on new or expanded data centers.

Washington had a fix. The Ratepayer Protection Act passed the House 417–3. Then, on Sept 30, it got 57 Senate votes, three short of 60. All 53 Republicans and four Democrats voted yes; 41 Democrats and two independents voted no.

The catch: the bill only asks state regulators to “consider” making data centers of 100+ megawatts pay the full cost of the grid upgrades they need. Chuck Schumer called it “toothless.” And in New Hampshire, Republican Gov. Kelly Ayotte plans a statewide data center moratorium.

Our take: both parties say data centers should pay. Make it mandatory, then pass it.
