Peak Energy raises $108M

Peak Energy, which makes passively cooled sodium-ion batteries for grid energy storage, raised an oversubscribed $108 million Series B led by General Motors Ventures, with Maverick Silicon, Infinite Capital, Eclipse, Xora Innovation and LG Technology Ventures participating. The three-year-old company says it has more than $1 billion in customer orders and is expanding into data centers. “Peak’s mission from day one has been to lower the cost of energy through safer, simpler, cleaner, and more reliable technology,” says co-founder and CEO Landon Mossburg.
