This startup was worth $60M a year ago. Now it’s a unicorn.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 23, 2026, 9 a.m. PT

Blacksmith raised a $66 million Series C led by Sequoia that values it at $1 billion. It comes two months after a $45 million Series B at a $550 million valuation, and a year after a $10 million Series A at $60 million. It has raised $125 million to date.

Blacksmith helps companies build and test software, including code written by AI. It says it has run over 90 million CI jobs, or automated code checks, for customers including Mercury and Granola, and that its annual recurring revenue has grown 17x. “As agents write more of the world’s software, the question that matters is whether it can be trusted,” said co-founder and CEO Aditya Jayaprakash.
