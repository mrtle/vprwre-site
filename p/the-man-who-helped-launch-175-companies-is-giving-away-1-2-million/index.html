The man who helped launch 175+ companies is giving away 1.2 million startup ideas.

Swipe to see why the man who helped launch 175+ companies is giving away 1.2 million startup ideas.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 20, 2026, 6:19 a.m. PT

Bill Gross, who founded the tech incubator Idealab in 1996, launched Idealab.ai, a platform that gives away more than 1.2 million AI-generated business ideas. Its AI scans more than 17 million sources, from news and research to patents and company filings, looking for unmet needs and new technology.

Each idea comes with startup costs, time to a first customer, competitors and a 90-day plan. The ideas are free to search and use; some add-on services are paid.

Cloudflare, the first partner in Idealab.ai’s “Periodic Table of Business Functions,” is offering up to $100,000 in credits to companies built on the platform. The table’s 118 business functions roll out through 2027.

“We want to give those ideas away and help people build them,” Gross said.
