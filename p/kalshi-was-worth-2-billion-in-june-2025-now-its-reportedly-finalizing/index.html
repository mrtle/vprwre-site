This app lets people trade on real-world events. It was worth $2 billion in June 2025. Now it’s reportedly finalizing a round at about $40 billion.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 6, 2026, 4 a.m. PT

Kalshi is a prediction market founded in 2018 by Tarek Mansour and Luana Lopes Lara, who met at MIT. Forbes has called her the world’s youngest self-made woman billionaire.

The twist: Kalshi got regulated first. The CFTC approved it as an exchange in 2020, and when the agency blocked its election contracts, Kalshi sued and won.

Bloomberg says Sequoia and Wellington are in talks to lead the new round, and that an IPO could come as soon as 2027. Kalshi hasn’t confirmed the round.

It’s not over: most of its volume is sports, and states say that’s gambling. Kalshi has won in one federal appeals court and lost in two, and New Jersey has asked the Supreme Court to step in.

Our take: Kalshi’s edge was getting regulated before scaling. Now that bet faces its biggest test.
