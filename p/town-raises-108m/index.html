Town raises $108M

Town, a San Francisco startup that gives every employee their own AI assistant, raised a $108 million Series B co-led by Forerunner Ventures and Menlo Ventures at a $1 billion valuation. Andreessen Horowitz, First Round Capital, Serena Ventures and others joined. Its assistants, called Townies, connect to email, calendars and work tools, and can now hand off work to each other and deal with customers and partners by email and text. Town says it reached seven figures in annual recurring revenue within weeks of its June launch, about four months before this round.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 20, 2026, 5:42 a.m. PT

FUN FACT: Town started out in 2025 as an AI tax-prep service for small businesses. CEO Jean-Denis Greze’s own Townie is a silver fox named Ivy, a nod to his prematurely grey hair, per Fortune.

FOUNDERS: Jean-Denis Greze (CEO; former CTO of Plaid) and Tony Vincent (formerly ran product for applied AI at Google)

INVESTORS: Forerunner Ventures and Menlo Ventures (co-leads), Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), First Round Capital, Serena Ventures, Better Tomorrow Ventures, Precedent.vc, Scribble Ventures, Sparked Ventures

AMOUNT: $108,000,000

HQ: San Francisco, California
