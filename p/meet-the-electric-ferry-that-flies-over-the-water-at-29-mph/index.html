Meet the electric ferry that flies over the water at 29 mph.

This ferry doesn’t float. It flies.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 6, 2026, 2:30 p.m. PT

Auckland startup Vessev builds the VS-9, a 29-foot electric boat whose underwater wings lift the hull out of the water at speed. Less drag means less energy: the company says it’s about three times more efficient than conventional vessels.

It’s not a concept. A VS-9 began carrying passengers with Fullers360 in Auckland in 2025, and TechCrunch just rode one in New York.

The twist: Vessev may not just sell boats. TechCrunch reports it may eventually sell hydrofoil kits to other boatbuilders, and CEO Eric Laakmann says batteries “have been commoditized.”

In August, Vessev raised $19 million in a round led by Blackbird. Its first U.S. customer, FlyTahoe on Lake Tahoe, is waiting on permits.

Our take: electric cars fought physics with bigger batteries. Vessev fights it by getting out of the water.
