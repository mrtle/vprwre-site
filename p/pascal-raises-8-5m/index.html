Pascal raises $8.5M

Pascal, a Massachusetts startup building solid-state cooling, raised an $8.5 million follow-on to its seed round, co-led by Sazze Partners and LB Investment with KDB Silicon Valley participating. Instead of a conventional refrigerant, its system pumps carbon dioxide into cartridges of a proprietary sponge-like material that warms up as the gas works its way in and absorbs heat when the process runs in reverse. CEO Adam Slavney says a fridge or heat pump using it should be around 20% more efficient.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 22, 2026, 5 p.m. PT

FUN FACT: The idea came from a surprise in a Harvard lab: swapping one gas for another in a high-pressure test changed the results. “Why are these things different?” Slavney recalls asking.

FOUNDERS: Adam Slavney (co-founder and CEO), Jinyoung Seo (co-founder and CTO)

INVESTORS: Sazze Partners, LB Investment, KDB Silicon Valley

AMOUNT: $8,500,000

NEXT: A pilot with a multinational beverage company, a first prototype early next year and a batch of refrigerators in 2028
