Why did Disney hire the CEO of the AI app it threatened?

Disney threatened an AI chatbot app. Then it hired the app’s CEO.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 5, 2026, 8 p.m. PT

On Sept 18, Disney named Karandeep Anand, CEO of chatbot app Character.AI, its first-ever chief technology officer. He reports to CEO Josh D’Amaro and started Oct 2.

The twist: Disney’s lawyers sent Character.AI a cease-and-desist on Sept 18, 2025, one year to the day earlier, accusing it of “blatantly infringing” Disney’s copyrights, Variety reported. The app pulled the characters.

Disney has sent cease-and-desists to Character.AI, Meta and Google, agreed to a $1B OpenAI stake it later dropped, and now hired a chatbot CEO. In late September, a PhotoPass test at Magic Kingdom’s Haunted Mansion turned guests into stone busts. Fans call it generative AI; Disney hasn’t confirmed the tech.

Our take: Disney’s fight was over who owns Elsa, never the tech. But if a photo op turns your face into a statue, Disney should say what made it and where your photo goes.

Disney guards Elsa. Who guards your face?
