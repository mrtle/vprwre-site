Tesla’s profit fell 43%. Musk is betting on robots.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 22, 2026, 2 p.m. PT

Tesla’s third-quarter revenue was flat at $28.05 billion, and GAAP net income fell 43% to $787 million. Operating income dropped 56% to $718 million, a 2.6% margin, as research and development spending jumped 69% to $2.76 billion. Deliveries slipped 2.1% to 486,532, and regulatory credit sales fell 73% to $113 million.

The bets: active Full Self-Driving subscriptions, a count that includes upfront purchases, rose 67% to 1.74 million. Robotaxis reached nearly 2 million cumulative unsupervised miles, and Cybercab joined the Austin fleet. Musk said he remains convinced Optimus “will be the biggest product on Earth” and expects deliveries to outside companies around the end of 2027. Tesla spent $4.73 billion on capital expenditures in the quarter and now expects about $20 billion for the year, down from more than $25 billion.

Tesla also took a $142 million paper loss on its SpaceX stake, partly offset by a Bitcoin gain of about $290 million. It wouldn’t take questions on a possible SpaceX merger on the call. Shares fell 2.2% after hours, to $377.88.
