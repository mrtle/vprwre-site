Two brothers and a Thiel Fellow just raised $1 billion.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 22, 2026, 11 a.m. PT

Flapping Airplanes, a year-old AI lab, raised a $1 billion Series A co-led by Index and Lux, reportedly at a $6 billion valuation. Earlier backers Sequoia, GV and Kleiner Perkins took part, and Valor, Atreides, A* and Nvidia are also investors. Nine months ago it launched with a $180 million seed round from GV, Sequoia and Index that valued it at $1.5 billion.

The lab wants to train large AI models on far less data, “without ingesting half the internet.” Its founders say they’ve built “the world’s most complete megakernel framework,” software that can run training and inference side by side so GPUs are rarely sitting idle.

FOUNDERS: Brothers Asher Spector (Stanford statistics PhD) and Ben Spector (former Stanford computer science PhD student, founded the student-run accelerator Prod at MIT), and Aidan Smith (Thiel Fellow, worked at Neuralink in college)
