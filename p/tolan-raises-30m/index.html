Tolan raises $30M

Tolan, a San Francisco startup behind an AI companion app, raised $30 million from Union Square Ventures, Khosla Ventures and Homebrew, doubling its total funding. Users talk to a cartoon alien they name and customize, which helps them with daily life, relationships and goals. The company says about 250,000 people talk to their Tolan every month, roughly 70% of them women and 40% parents, and that its annual recurring revenue is in the double-digit millions. It didn’t disclose a valuation.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 20, 2026, 5:46 a.m. PT

FUN FACT: The company used to be called Portola, and its aliens come from “the Planet Portola.” It also hired science-fiction novelist Eliot Peper as its head of story.

FOUNDERS: Quinten Farmer (CEO; co-founder of Even, sold to Walmart’s fintech venture in 2022), Evan Goldschmidt (CTO) and Ajay Mehta (President)

INVESTORS: Union Square Ventures, Khosla Ventures, Homebrew

AMOUNT: $30,000,000

HQ: San Francisco, California
