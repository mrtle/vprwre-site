These $399 robot ducks just passed 20,000 preorders.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 25, 2026, 1 p.m. PT

During his keynote at Nvidia’s GTC Berlin conference, Jensen Huang played with a flock of Microducks on stage: they stomped their feet, made R2-D2-like noises, sat, fell over for a “nap” and did somersaults on voice command. The next day, Hugging Face’s Clem Delangue said preorders had passed 20,000 units, and that Microduck might become the year’s best-selling robot.

Microduck is a $399 open-source biped about 25 centimeters tall, with 15 motors, a built-in camera, a small LiDAR sensor and a beak that can pick up objects. It was trained in simulation with Nvidia’s technology and ships knowing basic moves like walking, sitting and standing, and owners can teach it new tricks and share them with the community. It’s made by Pollen Robotics, which Hugging Face acquired in 2025; Nvidia agreed in September to buy Hugging Face, a deal still under regulatory review. Hugging Face aims to ship the first orders before Christmas in the US.
