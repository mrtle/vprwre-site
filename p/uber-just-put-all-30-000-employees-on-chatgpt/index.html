Uber just put all 30,000 employees on ChatGPT.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 23, 2026, 5 p.m. PT

Uber is rolling out ChatGPT Work, the enterprise AI agent OpenAI introduced in July, to its entire workforce of roughly 30,000 people. It follows a pilot earlier this year with 1,000 employees, about 80% of whom weren’t engineers, according to Sumanth Sukumar, Uber’s vice president of engineering.

In the pilot, employees used it to analyze operations data, localize training materials and build personalized dashboards. Uber also paired 30 AI engineers with teams for two weeks in what it calls “AI pods.” Uber hasn’t disclosed how much it’s spending, and says it will track how employees use it.
