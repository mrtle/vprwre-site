Different raises $48M

Different, an AI marketing startup, came out of stealth with $48 million led by General Catalyst, with Lightspeed Venture Partners participating. Its system runs B2B demand generation end to end: marketers set goals, budget and brand rules and approve campaigns, while Different suggests target accounts, builds personalized ads, emails and landing pages, launches them across channels and learns from every result. The team spent about a year refining it with early customers.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 21, 2026, 12 p.m. PT

FUN FACT: At early customer Descope, two marketers now run up to seven programs at once, reaching 2,800 target accounts.

FOUNDERS: Ajeet Singh (co-founder and CEO; co-founded Nutanix and ThoughtSpot), building it with Abhishek Pani and Abhinav Yajurvedi

INVESTORS: General Catalyst, Lightspeed Venture Partners

AMOUNT: $48,000,000

TEAMS: San Francisco and Bangalore
