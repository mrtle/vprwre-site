The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 21, 2026, 5 p.m. PT

Daytona raised a $60 million Series B at a $1 billion valuation, led by FirstMark Growth, bringing its total funding to $91 million. Angel investors include Patrick Collison (Stripe), Tobi Lütke (Shopify), Aravind Srinivas (Perplexity), Eric Yuan (Zoom), Olivier Pomel (Datadog), Adam D’Angelo (Quora) and Brendan Foody (Mercor). Qualcomm Ventures, Samsung Next and Okta Ventures joined as strategic investors.

Daytona runs isolated sandboxes where AI agents can run code, use tools and finish tasks, on Linux, Windows, macOS and GPU machines. Customers include Meta, Cohere, Writer, Clay, n8n and Postman. Writer spun up 300,000 sandboxes on Daytona in a single month, and the company says its revenue has grown about 25 times since January.

CEO and co-founder Ivan Burazin’s background includes Codeanywhere and the Shift developer conference, and he was chief developer experience officer at Infobip.
