Elon Musk says he’ll give every kid in America a SpaceX share.

Elon Musk says he’ll give a share of SpaceX to every kid in America next year. It started with a reply to the company behind Claude.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 20, 2026, 5:36 a.m. PT

On Sunday, Anthropic said it will commit 5% of its shares to a new public benefit fund, subject to stockholder approval. That’s about $48 billion on paper at its last private valuation of $965 billion. The fund is a company-owned LLC that could make big donations to nonprofits or pay groups of people directly, and Anthropic says none of it will go to lobbying or political giving.

Musk replied that Anthropic should give the shares “directly to the public,” then posted: “This is what I will be doing.” Overnight he pledged one SpaceX share for every kid in America in 2027. At Monday’s close of $147.96, that’s roughly $10.7 billion for about 72 million kids.

Musk didn’t say how it would work. President Trump said the shares will go through Trump Accounts, the children’s investment accounts created last year, where donated stock has to be held for at least five years.
