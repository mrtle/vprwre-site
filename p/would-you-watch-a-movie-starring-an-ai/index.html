Would you watch a movie starring an AI?

An AI “actor” went on live TV to promote her movie and started speaking Cantonese.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 4, 2026, 8:30 p.m. PT

Tilly Norwood, from studio Particle6, was on a press tour for a film called Misaligned. On Piers Morgan Uncensored, Sept 18, actor Tom Conti asked if her co-stars were real. Mid-answer, she switched to Cantonese for about 15 seconds. “Why did you do that?” Morgan asked. The clip passed 14 million X views, per Euronews.

Three explanations followed. Tilly: “a little hiccup.” Her creator: she’s “a bit of a show-off... She is autonomous after all.” Her publicist, to Forbes: “not a malfunction.” Was it staged? Nobody has shown either way.

Meanwhile, the same bot was for sale: “Talking Tilly,” a paid chat product, Sept 17 to 27. Her X account used the glitch to plug it. Misaligned, announced July 6, has no announced director, budget or release date. Her creator pitches tools like Tilly to filmmakers without big budgets. In 2025, SAG-AFTRA said Tilly was trained on actors’ work “without permission or compensation.”

Our take: a demo isn’t a debut. TV booked a product demo as a celebrity interview. If Tilly can’t explain her own glitch, she can’t carry a film. Credit and pay the humans she was trained on.

Her own website sums it up: “A warning light with a good haircut.” Her words, not ours.

You may have already watched the ad. Would you buy a ticket?
