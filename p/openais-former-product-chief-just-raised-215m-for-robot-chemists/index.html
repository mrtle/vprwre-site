OpenAI’s former product chief just raised $215M for robot chemists.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 24, 2026, 1 p.m. PT

Kevin Weil, previously chief product officer and VP of science at OpenAI, launched Warp Sciences out of stealth with $215 million led by Lightspeed. Weil says the round values the company at $865 million. Premji Invest, Mayfield, IVP, Lux, Conviction, Menlo Ventures and others participated, and angels include Brian Armstrong, Mike Krieger, Fidji Simo, Sarah Friar, Mark Chen and Dylan Field.

Warp is building a US-based chemistry service that combines chemists, AI and robotics to make and test the molecules drug researchers need, starting with analog synthesis for lead optimization. Chemists will wear cameras and sensor gloves to train the robots on lab tasks, and Warp says it won’t develop drugs of its own. Weil hopes to bring back chemistry work that biotech companies often outsource to contractors in China. His co-founders are chief scientific officer Sol Reisberg, CTO Mano Paluri, formerly a VP at Meta Superintelligence Labs, and COO DJ Patil, the first US chief data scientist.
