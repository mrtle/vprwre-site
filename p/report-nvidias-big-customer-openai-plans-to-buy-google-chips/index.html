Report: Nvidia’s big customer OpenAI plans to buy Google chips.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 28, 2026, 3 p.m. PT

OpenAI plans to buy TPUs, Google’s custom AI chips, The Information reports. Financial terms weren’t disclosed. OpenAI has rented Google’s TPUs since 2025, Reuters reported; this would be a purchase.

OpenAI’s own careers page points the same way. It’s hiring a “Software Engineer, TPU Compilers” to “lead the optimization of OpenAI’s state-of-the-art frontier models on TPUs.” The listed pay: $380,000 to $500,000, plus equity.

Why Nvidia should care: Nvidia invested $30 billion in OpenAI earlier this year. A 2025 plan to invest up to $100 billion is “probably not in the cards,” CEO Jensen Huang said in March.

Our take: OpenAI isn’t dropping Nvidia. But adding Google to its chip suppliers gives it more leverage on price and supply.
