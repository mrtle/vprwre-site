Mach9 raises $34M

Mach9, which makes AI CAD software for civil engineering, raised a $34 million Series A co-led by First Round Capital and Bessemer Venture Partners, with Prudence and existing investor Quiet Capital participating. Its in-house AI models turn laser scans and imagery of roads and sites into first-draft CAD plans, which it says are centimeter-accurate, that engineers then check and refine. Co-founder Alex Baikovitz says doing that by hand in legacy software takes about a week per mile; Mach9 says it’s 10x faster.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 22, 2026, 1 p.m. PT

FUN FACT: Mach9 started in 2021 as Mach9 Robotics, building inspection hardware, until customers said they didn’t need better devices. “The real pain was in their workflows,” Baikovitz says.

FOUNDER: Alex Baikovitz (co-founder)

INVESTORS: First Round Capital, Bessemer Venture Partners, Prudence, Quiet Capital

AMOUNT: $34,000,000

TRACTION: Nearly 100 private-beta customers, 2,500+ projects
