The US has ordered DeepSeek off government computers.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 21, 2026, 3 p.m. PT

The Department of Homeland Security published an order in the Federal Register on Oct. 20 excluding DeepSeek’s products and services from federal civilian agency purchases and removing them from civilian agency IT systems. It also covers third-party products and services built using DeepSeek. Agencies can request exceptions. The order itself dates to June.

DHS says DeepSeek’s models present “multiple threat vectors.” Data sent to its China-hosted models is stored where Chinese law gives the government “wide latitude to request and obtain data from domestic companies,” and could be used to “produce actionable intelligence on U.S. persons and/or government programs.”

A law passed in December 2025 already restricted DeepSeek in national security systems; this order extends restrictions to civilian agencies. It doesn’t cover personal use. China’s foreign ministry said Beijing opposes stretching the concept of national security and politicizing tech and trade issues.
