Halluminate raises $30M

Halluminate, a San Francisco startup that builds practice simulations where AI models learn finance work, raised a $30 million Series A led by Oak HC/FT, with participation from Y Combinator, Orange Collective, FT Partners and Heavybit. It tests where AI models fall short on finance tasks, like due diligence for an acquisition, then builds simulated environments for them to train on. The round brings its total raised to $38.5 million.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 6, 2026, 5:30 p.m. PT

FUN FACT: It’s a nine-person team, per Fortune, and CEO Jerry Wu says Halluminate is already profitable, with a mid-eight-figure annual revenue run rate. He says four of the top five closed-source U.S. AI labs are paying customers.

FOUNDERS: Jerry Wu (CEO) and Wyatt Marshall (CTO)

INVESTORS: Oak HC/FT (lead), Y Combinator, Orange Collective, FT Partners, Heavybit, plus individual researchers from Anthropic, OpenAI and Meta (per Fortune)

AMOUNT: $30,000,000

HQ: San Francisco, California
