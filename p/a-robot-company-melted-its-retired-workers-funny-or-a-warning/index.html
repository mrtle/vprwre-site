A robot company melted its retired workers. Funny or a warning?

A robot company asked the internet how to retire its old humanoids, and the Terminator answered.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 5, 2026, 7 p.m. PT

On Aug 19, Figure CEO Brett Adcock posted that his F.02 robots deserved “a proper sendoff.” Arnold Schwarzenegger replied on X: “You should melt them.” Figure says the F.02s loaded 90,000+ parts in 11 months at a BMW plant.

On Sept 30, Figure posted the film: its robots leaping into a 75-ton electric arc furnace in Imatra, Finland. Figure says melting protects its intellectual property, and that no foundry it asked in the U.S. or Mexico would take robots with lithium-ion batteries.

The twist: the scrap went on sale. Figure is preselling “artifacts” from the melted metal at $500 and $1,900, and the big one is sold out. Figure hasn’t said how many robots it melted, and its claims about the jumps haven’t been independently checked.

Our take: funny, yes. Also an ad. The warning is the speed: 11 months on a BMW line, then the furnace.
