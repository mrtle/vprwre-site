Should Tesla and SpaceX become one $3.5 trillion company?

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 21, 2026, 9 a.m. PT

Elon Musk confirmed on SpaceX’s third-quarter earnings call Tuesday that the boards of SpaceX and Tesla have each formed a special committee of independent directors to review a possible merger. He said a deal “would be a good idea,” but called himself a “conflicted party” who can’t take part in the talks. No terms have been agreed.

The numbers: SpaceX, which went public in June, is worth more than $2 trillion, and Tesla nearly $1.5 trillion. SpaceX also reported $14.6 billion in quarterly revenue, with AI now 52% of its income. Musk points to shared work on AI, chips and robots, including Terafab, a joint chip-making project.

The catch: a deal needs a majority of all outstanding Tesla shares, not just the votes cast, which Musk called “quite a high hurdle.” In November 2025, a nonbinding vote on Tesla investing in Musk’s xAI failed because abstentions counted as no votes. Tesla invested $2 billion anyway.
