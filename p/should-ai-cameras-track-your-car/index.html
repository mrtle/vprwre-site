Should AI cameras track your car?

AI cameras are reading license plates across the U.S. Now the left and the right both want limits.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 5, 2026, 7:30 p.m. PT

Flock Safety says it has 120,000 of these cameras in 49 states, and police can search what they record. On Sept 30, Republican Sen. Josh Hawley unveiled the Stop Flock Abuse Act: police would delete driver data after 10 days. On Oct 2, Democratic Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez and Sens. Bernie Sanders and Jeff Merkley unveiled the Ban Flock Act. It would bar federal use and withhold some federal grants unless states and towns ban plate readers.

A woman told senators a Flock camera put her black Durango near a fatal crash; she spent 13 days in jail before the case was dropped.

The twist: Flock told Hawley that privacy shouldn’t “depend on one vendor’s good judgment,” KCUR reported.

Our take: keep the camera, lose the dragnet. Require a warrant to look back, and delete the rest in days.
