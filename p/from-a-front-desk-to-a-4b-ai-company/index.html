From a front desk to a $4B AI company.

She took a front-desk job to learn real estate. Now her AI company is valued at $4 billion.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 5, 2026, 11:30 p.m. PT

Minna Song’s front-desk job was at a Manhattan real estate firm, Forbes reports; she visited dozens of apartment buildings to learn what renters and managers deal with. In 2017 she cofounded EliseAI with Tony Stoyanov.

The twist: it was Plan B. The two met at Cambridge, and their first startup was in influencer marketing. “We hated it,” she told Forbes.

EliseAI’s AI answers renters’ texts, calls and emails for landlords. It says it’s used by one in six US apartments and passed $200M in annual recurring revenue. On Sept 29 it announced $350M at a $4B valuation, led by a16z and Bessemer.

Our take: work the job before you automate it.

If AI runs the leasing office, who should get the savings: landlords or renters?
