ChatGPT now lets you lock your chats.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 22, 2026, 4 p.m. PT

OpenAI is rolling out Locked Chats, which put ChatGPT conversations behind a four-digit PIN, or Face ID or Touch ID on supported devices. It’s meant for personal conversations, or planning a surprise, when someone else might see your screen or device.

Locked chats move to a separate “Locked Chats” section, apart from regular history, pinned chats and search results. With Memory on, they get their own memory, so new details from them don’t flow into regular chats. Resetting the PIN permanently deletes all the chats, files and other content locked behind the old one.

What it isn’t: locking doesn’t add end-to-end encryption or a separate encrypted vault, change how long OpenAI keeps the chat, or change your “Improve the model for everyone” setting. It’s coming to Free, Go, Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu plans, but not ChatGPT for Healthcare, and you can’t lock temporary, archived, group or shared-link chats.
