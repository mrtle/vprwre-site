The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 26, 2026, 9 a.m. PT

On Oct. 22, David Im launched Sume API, which he calls the first image and video model provider with 0% markup. It says it sells AI models like ByteDance’s Seedance at exactly what the model maker charges, through a web studio, an API and an MCP connection for tools like Claude and ChatGPT. His launch post has more than 800,000 views.

His claim: the big platforms mark up the same models. Sume says Runway adds 414% on Seedance 2.0 at 480p, fal adds 100%, and Higgsfield’s API adds 40% even at its biggest advertised discount. On Sume’s own chart, Seedance 2.5 at 1080p costs $0.5686 a second, versus $1.1640 on fal and $1.1373 on Higgsfield’s API (list prices, before Higgsfield’s discounts).

Hours later, Im posted an X article titled “F*** Higgsfield: why we launched a 0% markup API.” His argument: AI video apps all offer the same models behind the same interface, so routing margins will get competed away, and the real moat is owning a company’s workflow. He says Sume plans to make money later from workflow software, not from the models.

Not everyone is convinced. One user’s AI check of Sume’s docs found a 25% markup and a $40 monthly plan, and Im replied that it was a deprecated doc. ArtRealm AI found the biggest savings are on Seedance and a few image models, while many others cost the same as on fal.

Sume was started in 2026 by David Im and Chase Huh and is backed by Founders, Inc.
