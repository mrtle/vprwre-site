Ghost raises $11M

Ghost, a San Francisco startup led by 19-year-old CEO Zain Javaid, raised an $11 million seed round led by Andreessen Horowitz, with Abstract Ventures, Audacious Ventures, SV Angel and others joining. Its product, Core, is a $3,499 personal AI computer built to run AI models and agents on-device. It connects to a user’s apps, files and devices so it can act as an assistant and take actions on their behalf. Preorders opened Oct. 5, and Ghost says the first batch ships Oct. 31.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 6, 2026, 5 p.m. PT

FUN FACT: Two of Ghost’s four founders are 19. Ghost currently builds Cores at its San Francisco facility, and Javaid says new AI models can be pushed to the device over the air, “similar to how Tesla works.”

FOUNDERS: Zain Javaid (CEO), Nicholas Chua, Yifei Chen and Gautam Sharda

INVESTORS: Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), Abstract Ventures, Audacious Ventures, SV Angel, Nova

AMOUNT: $11,000,000

HQ: San Francisco, California
