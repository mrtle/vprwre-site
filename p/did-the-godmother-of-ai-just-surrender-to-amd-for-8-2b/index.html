Did the ‘godmother of AI’ just surrender to AMD for $8.2B?

The scientist many call the ‘godmother of AI’ just agreed to sell her startup to chipmaker AMD for about $8.2 billion.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 5, 2026, 10 p.m. PT

Fei-Fei Li built ImageNet, the image database that helped spark modern AI. In 2024 she co-founded World Labs to build “world models”: AI that makes interactive 3D worlds for games, film and robot training.

The twist: her buyer was her backer. AMD invested in World Labs’ $1B round in February, and AMD CEO Lisa Su was an early investor, Li wrote.

The Sept 28 deal is all stock and hasn’t closed: regulators must sign off, and AMD expects it to close by the end of 2026. Li would become AMD’s chief scientist, reporting to Su.

Our take: it’s a surrender of independence, maybe a smart one. Li says AI without a focused hardware effort is “hobbled in efficiency.”

Is selling to your own backer a surrender, or the only way to win?
