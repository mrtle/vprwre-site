Deals

Sequoia turned a roughly $60 million bet on WhatsApp into about $3 billion.

Facebook turned down two friends for jobs. Then it agreed to pay $19 billion for the app they built.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 6, 2026, 3 a.m. PT

Brian Acton and Jan Koum, both ex-Yahoo, were Facebook rejects. In 2009, Koum incorporated WhatsApp on his birthday, and Acton joined.

The twist: they didn’t want venture money. Sequoia’s Jim Goetz spent eight months just getting them to talk. In 2011 they took $8 million. In 2013, Sequoia secretly added $50 million at a $1.5 billion valuation.

When Facebook bought WhatsApp in 2014, Sequoia’s roughly $60 million was reportedly worth about $3 billion, around 50x and more than double the fund its first check came from.

Our take: a rejection is data, not a verdict.
