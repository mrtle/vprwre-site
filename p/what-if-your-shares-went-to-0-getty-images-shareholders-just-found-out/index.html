What if your shares went to $0? Getty Images shareholders just found out.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 23, 2026, 8:45 a.m. PT

Getty filed for Chapter 11 in Delaware on Oct 21. Its library holds more than 662 million images and videos.

Under the plan, Getty family members and affiliates of Koch Equity Development put in up to $533M and own 100%. More than $500M of debt is wiped out.

Existing shares are canceled. Shareholders get nothing.
