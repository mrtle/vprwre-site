Whop’s sellers generate $550M a month. Now it’s worth $5.1B.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 22, 2026, 3 p.m. PT

Whop raised a $200 million Series D at a $5.1 billion valuation, led by Sequoia and Shopify Ventures. It’s the company’s second raise this year, after a $200 million strategic investment from Tether at a $1.6 billion valuation in February.

Whop gives business operators a gamified app and an API for distribution, starting businesses and getting paid. Roughly 25,000 operators generate $550 million on Whop each month, and Whop says it facilitates more than $7 billion in on-platform transactions for people in 185 countries. Businesses on it range from invoicing software for trucking companies to emergency tree removal and private jet booking, and some operators are 16-year-old first-time entrepreneurs. “We’re helping humanity create businesses for people who can’t find a job,” said Sequoia’s Doug Leone.

Whop, led by co-founder and CEO Steven Schwartz, is headquartered in Palo Alto with offices in New York.
