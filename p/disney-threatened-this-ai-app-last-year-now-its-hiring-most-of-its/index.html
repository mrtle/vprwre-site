Last year, Disney threatened this AI chatbot app. Now it’s hiring most of its staff.

The Take By VPRWRE Oct. 20, 2026, 6:15 a.m. PT

Character.AI lets people chat with AI characters, most of them made by other users, and about 12 million people use it every month. On Oct. 12, the company behind it filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy to sell its assets, citing “significant legal, regulatory, and operational headwinds.” It was losing $8 million to $9 million a month.

The twist: Disney made the opening bid. In September 2025, Disney sent Character.AI a cease-and-desist letter over chatbots that imitated its characters. This September, it named Karandeep Anand, who had run Character.AI as CEO since June 2025, as its first-ever chief technology officer.

For $20 million, Disney gets a permanent, non-exclusive license to some of Character.AI’s technology and patents, but not the app, the brand or users’ chats. It also agreed to offer jobs to at least 60 employees, most of the staff. In 2024, Google paid about $2.7 billion to license the startup’s technology and bring back its founders.

A judge hears Disney’s bid on Oct. 30, and rivals can still outbid it. Would you talk to an AI character made by Disney? Tell us below.
